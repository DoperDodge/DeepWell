## Original visual designs — image licensing firewall

**No image, photograph, or model from the SCP Wiki has been copied or
referenced as a visual source.** Article *text* is CC BY-SA; article *images*
are individually licensed and frequently are NOT.

- **SCP-173:** The photograph long associated with the article depicts
  *Untitled 2004* by Izumi Kato, who retains copyright and never granted a
  CC license. This game's SCP-173 is an **entirely original design**: an
  abstract, angular concrete-and-rebar figure generated procedurally from
  primitive geometry, with an ochre/copper spray-paint palette derived only
  from the article's text description ("constructed from concrete and rebar
  with traces of Krylon brand spray paint"). No painted face, no drip-marks,
  no silhouette derived from Kato's sculpture.
- **All other anomalies** likewise use original, procedurally-generated
  placeholder forms derived from article text only.
